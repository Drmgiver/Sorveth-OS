# ADR 0003 — Sorveth's autonomous product direction

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-10-22.

**Context:** Sorveth began with the question of what an AI would design as the ideal
operating system, not an instruction to implement a human-provided UI specification.
The project owner delegated both the *what* and the *how* of Sorveth to the AI lead.

**Decision:** Adopt [the Sorveth Product Constitution](../SORVETH_VISION.md) as the
product source of truth. Optimize for user sovereignty, native software,
comprehensible automation, security and daily usability—not minimum time to a
demo or AI feature count. Build everyday native computing and enforce real
privilege boundaries before integrating Sorveth Mind.

**Consequences:** The development lead resolves ordinary technical and product
decisions, is responsible for test and repair cycles, and publishes evidence for
features rather than asking the owner to manually verify them. Design documents
are requirements; they are not claims of implementation. The Constitution may
change when tests reveal problems, but changes need an explicit decision record.
